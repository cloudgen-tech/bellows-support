Bellows privacy policy

Provider: CLOUDGENTECH LLC
Effective October 25, 2026

What Bellows collects

Bellows does not collect or send your photographs, camera preview, settings, or library to CloudGen Tech. The app includes no advertising, analytics, tracking, account registration, or remote image processing.

Camera and local storage

Camera access is used to make photographs. Originals, developed images, thumbnails, print-library records, and preferences are stored within the app on your iPhone. Bellows does not request microphone or location access. Device backups may include app data according to your iOS backup settings.

Saving and sharing

Photo Library add access is requested only when you choose to save a photograph to Photos. Sharing uses the iOS share sheet. The destination you choose handles that shared content under its own policies.

Purchases

Apple handles App Store purchases and payment details. Bellows does not receive your payment-card details and does not require an account.

Your controls

You can manage Camera and Photos permissions in iOS Settings. Prints remain in the local light table; Bellows has no tray or in-app delete control. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data; images separately saved to Photos or shared elsewhere remain in those destinations. Your iOS backup settings govern any backup copies.

This website and support

This support site is hosted by GitHub Pages and does not include analytics scripts or tracking cookies added by CloudGen Tech. GitHub may process technical information needed to host and protect the site under the GitHub privacy statement. If you open a support issue, its contents are public and handled by GitHub. Please do not post private photographs or personal details.

Privacy questions

Contact CloudGen Tech through the Bellows support tracker. For app questions, no photographs or account credentials are needed.
