Support for Bellows, the folding instant camera for iPhone Duo.

Contact support

Open a support request on GitHub. Include your iOS version and the steps that reproduce the issue. GitHub issues are public: please do not include private photographs, personal information, or account details.

Let the light in

Bellows requires iPhone Duo and iOS 27.1 or later. Allow Camera for Bellows in iOS Settings. If another app is using the camera, finish that session and return to Bellows.

Watch a print appear

Press the shutter and watch the paper slide out. A gentle shake or a tap on the print speeds up the reveal. It also develops automatically. Bellows creates digital prints; no physical printer or paper is needed.

Open the camera

Unfold for a larger workspace. Use the outer preview to help your subject compose. To use the optional fold shutter, arm it, open beyond 130°, then fold below 85°. Reopen to rearm.

Keep your photographs

Your light table holds prints and originals on your iPhone. Save to Photos is optional. Prints moved to the tray can be restored. Save a separate copy of photographs you want to keep before uninstalling Bellows.

Sound and feel

Camera settings includes controls for mechanical sound, haptics, grain, and date imprint. Sounds respect Silent Mode. Reduce Motion uses a quieter print transition.

One purchase

Bellows is a one-time paid download with no subscription or in-app purchases. Apple manages purchases, redownloads, and refund requests.
